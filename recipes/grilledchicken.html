Grilled Chicken

Fire Up The Grill

This Summer, get outside and throw some lean chicken on the grill.

You will be able to be the grill master following this recipe.

Ingredients

  • 4 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
  • ¼ cup lemon juice, plus wedges for serving
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons dried oregano or parsley
  • 1 teaspoon seasoning salt
  • ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon onion powder

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. Place 1 chicken breast between 2 sheets of plastic wrap or parchment paper on a cutting board. Using a meat mallet or a rolling pin, gently pound each breast to 1/2-inch thickness. Repeat with remaining chicken breasts.
  3. Place lemon juice, olive oil, dried oregano, seasoning salt, black pepper, and onion powder into a large zip-top bag; squeeze to combine marinade. Add chicken and press out as much air as possible before sealing the bag. Gently massage chicken to distribute marinade. Marinate chicken in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes or up to 12 hours.
  4. Preheat an outdoor grill for medium-high heat and lightly oil the grate.
  5. Remove chicken from marinade and place, smooth-side down, on the preheated grill; cook, covered, until no longer pink in the center and the juices run clear, about 5 minutes per side. An instant-read thermometer inserted into the center of chicken breast should read at least 165 degrees F (74 degrees C). Discard excess marinade.
  6. Transfer chicken to a cutting board and tent with aluminum foil. Let rest for 5 minutes.
